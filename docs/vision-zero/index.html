Crashes fell versus 2019. Deaths did not.

Use 2019 as the last normal pre-COVID year, not 2020. Vision Zero crashes peaked at 4,529 in 2017, then 4,372 in 2018 and 4,354 in 2019. 2025 is 3,411 — 9.3 a day, 21.7% below 2019. Fatalities in the companion file were 11 in 2019 and 14 in 2025. Those are not the same series: do not add 14 deaths into 3,411 crashes.

2020 is a COVID year. Do not use it as a baseline. Crashes fell 23.2% in 2020 (4,354 → 3,346) while deaths rose (11 → 14). Compare 2025 to 2019, then show 2020 as the shock.
2026 is not a full year. Fatalities and crashes end on different dates. Crashes run through 1 July 2026 (1,661 year-to-date, 9.1 a day). Fatalities run through 15 May 2026 (5 deaths). Do not annualize either figure, and do not treat them as the same cutoff.

Most crashes are motor vehicles. Most deaths are people walking.

In 2025, motor vehicles were 2,584 of 3,411 crashes (75.8%). Pedestrians were 571 (16.7%) and bicycles 256 (7.5%). Of 14 deaths that year, 11 were pedestrians and 3 were motor-vehicle mode (mode_type mv — the file does not split drivers, passengers, or motorcyclists). Across complete years 2015–2025, pedestrians are 92 of 156 deaths.

Do not mix deaths into the crash total. 3,411 crashes and 14 deaths in 2025 come from two files. Brief them side by side. Adding them is not a citywide total.

Crashes peak at 9 p.m., not the morning commute.

In 2025 the busiest hour is 9 p.m. (212 crashes). The quietest is 9 a.m. (52). Friday is heaviest (535); Thursday and Sunday are lightest (457). Tuesday is 469. August is the peak month (355); February is the lightest (217). This is the opposite clock from 311, which opens most cases at 8 a.m.

What Boston residents should know

These are City of Boston Vision Zero crash and fatality records, 2015 through mid-2026. This is not 911, and it is not the Boston Police incident file.

Most recorded crashes are motor vehicles. In 2025, 2,584 of 3,411 crashes were motor-vehicle mode, 571 were pedestrian mode, and 256 were bicycle mode.
Most people who die in this file were walking. 11 of 14 traffic deaths in 2025 were pedestrians. From 2015 through 2025, 92 of 156 deaths were pedestrians.
2026 is only part of a year. Crash records in this dump run through 1 July 2026 (1,661 so far). Fatality records run through 15 May (5 deaths). 2026 is not a full year.
