Boston public data analysis

Employee earnings

$2.46B gross in 2025 · 25,397 people · Education, Police, and Fire are 79% of payroll. Departments, hires, overtime, and top earners.

Fire incidents

60,096 runs in 2024 · fires −34% since 2012. Dorchester led fires every year. Cooking is the common fire; building fires are the costly ones.

Police incidents

81,162 reports in 2025 · serious violence −41% since 2016. B2 / B3 / C11 still hold most shooting victims. 2020 is COVID — skip it as a baseline.
