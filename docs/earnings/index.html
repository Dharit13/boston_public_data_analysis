How the city pays people

Education is the teaching rollup (12,806 people, $1.09B, median $92.5k, almost no OT). Police + Fire are 19% of people and 35% of pay, and 83% of overtime. All 20 citywide top earners are BPD.

Education + Police + Fire = 79% of 2025 payroll. Food, Transportation, Facilities, and BPS police stay outside Education. The city’s “Transportation” row is mostly BPS cab monitors (median $33.7k), not the MBTA.

What this data cannot support

These files are calendar-year gross rows. A person who worked 3 months and a person who worked 12 months both appear as one row. Overtime hours are not reported — only dollars. “Other” is a junk drawer. 2024’s retro spike will distort any naive year-over-year raise. MBTA pay is a different employer and is not in this dataset.

Column names were not stable (TOTAL EARNINGS vs TOTAL GROSS; four Quinn spellings). Money used four formats. 2017 stored some ZIPs as integers; all were padded to five digits. 2020 is the messiest file: 93 missing ZIPs, 115 rows where published total disagrees with the sum of parts by more than $1, and a non-breaking space that is not valid UTF-8.
